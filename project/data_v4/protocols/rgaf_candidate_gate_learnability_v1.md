# Candidate-level RGAF learnability audit v1

Status: frozen after all preregistered v8 results, B0 diagnostics, T+B0
exploration, and truth-conditioned transition-visibility diagnostics were
observed, but before any learned candidate-gate outer result was computed.

This is explicitly post-result exploratory method development. Results on the
existing 784 rows cannot become confirmatory evidence for a final method.

## 1. Question

The per-sample oracle shows that B0 and sequence/statistical experts are
complementary, but the stored `transition_visibility` field is target
conditioned and therefore illegal as an inference-time gate. This audit asks a
narrower question:

> Can a gate trained only from candidate-level, inference-observable support
> features learn a useful transition residual on a completely held-out source?

## 2. Frozen data and branches

- 784 formal future-3 rows, with outer LODO sources CTID, Attack Flow, and
  Stockpile;
- fixed 184-parent-technique vocabulary;
- exact committed DeepSeek B0 Top-5 ranking;
- the frozen A order-2/order-1/unigram relevance model.

For candidate `c`, B0 uses reciprocal rank for returned ranks 1--5 and zero
otherwise. Let `p_A(c|h)` be A relevance and `p_0(c)` its training unigram.
Transition evidence is

```text
e(c|h) = clip(log((p_A(c|h)+1e-9)/(p_0(c)+1e-9)), -4, 4) / 4
```

The primary candidate score is asymmetric:

```text
g(x,c) = sigmoid(w^T phi(x,c))
z_RGAF(c) = score_B0(c) + g(x,c) * e(c|h)
```

`g=0` exactly reproduces B0. No target-conditioned visibility field is read by
the feature builder or scorer.

## 3. Frozen legal features

For every one of the 184 candidates, `phi(x,c)` contains only:

1. `log1p` order-1 context support;
2. `log1p` order-2 context support;
3. `log1p` count of `(last,c)` in training targets;
4. `log1p` count of `(last2,last,c)` in training targets;
5. fraction of training sources supporting `(last,c)`;
6. fraction of training sources supporting `(last2,last,c)`;
7. one minus normalized entropy of A relevance;
8. B0 reciprocal-rank score for `c`;
9. absolute transition evidence;
10. A rank percentile for `c`;
11. `log1p(prefix_len)`.

The following are forbidden features: test targets, `transition_visibility`,
`target_label_visibility`, source ID, campaign ID, sample ID, future text, and
any metric computed against the target.

Features are standardized with means and standard deviations from gate-
training candidates only.

## 4. Gate fitting without stacking leakage

The gate minimizes deterministic pairwise logistic ranking loss. Every target
candidate is paired with the union of:

- A Top-20 candidates;
- B0 Top-5 candidates;
- unigram Top-10 candidates.

Targets are removed from the negative set. Gate-training features for each
campaign are generated by an A model fit after excluding that entire campaign.
Thus the gate never trains on in-sample A evidence that was constructed from
the same campaign targets.

Optimization is fixed full-batch Adam for 60 epochs, learning rate 0.05,
initial intercept `logit(0.1)`, all other weights zero, and no stochastic
sampling. L2 is chosen from `{0.001, 0.01, 0.1}`.

For each outer fold, the two outer-training sources alternate as inner gate
training and inner validation. L2 maximizes the source-equal mean of the two
inner validation campaign-macro NDCG@5 values. Exact ties choose the larger L2.
The outer held-out source is not used for selection or fitting.

## 5. Frozen controls

- `B0`: exact direct DeepSeek Top-5;
- `A`: exact frozen sequence relevance ranking;
- `UniformResidual`: `score_B0 + e`, equivalent to opening the residual for
  every candidate;
- `RGAF-Shuffle`: rotate all candidate-aligned transition evidence and support
  features by 37 vocabulary positions before gate fitting and evaluation,
  while leaving B0 candidate scores unchanged. It uses the same selected L2
  and training procedure as RGAF and receives no extra tuning.

The rotation preserves transition-score distributions but destroys their
alignment with candidate identities. `RGAF-Shuffle >= RGAF` would undermine a
transition-evidence mechanism interpretation.

## 6. Metrics and interpretation

Primary metric is source-equal campaign-macro NDCG@5. Secondary metrics are
Hit@5, Precision@5, and Recall@5. Uncertainty is a paired 2,000-replicate
campaign bootstrap with seed 20260807.

The audit records three levels without changing the negative v8 result:

1. **learnability signal:** RGAF point estimate exceeds B0 in both CTID and
   Attack Flow and source-equal overall, while RGAF exceeds RGAF-Shuffle;
2. **strong exploratory signal:** level 1 plus the RGAF-B0 95% CI lower bound
   is above zero;
3. **no learnability evidence:** RGAF does not exceed B0 overall, or
   RGAF-Shuffle is at least as high as RGAF overall.

No result on these 784 already-observed rows may be described as prospective,
preregistered confirmation, or final method validation.

## 7. Mechanical gates and outputs

- exact B0 Top-5 reproduction on all 784 rows;
- exact A Top-20 reproduction on all 784 rows;
- 784 unique sample IDs and 10/35/27 campaigns;
- no target-conditioned field passed into feature construction or scoring;
- output directory must not already exist;
- script, protocol, input, and output SHA-256 hashes are recorded.
